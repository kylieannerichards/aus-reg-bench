# results/analysis/

Notebooks and scripts that turn raw response logs into statistics and figures.

Methodological emphasis:

- Bayesian capability estimation (posterior intervals, not just point accuracy)
- Heavy-tail error analysis (worst-case behaviour, not just means)
- Inter-rater reliability where expert judgement is in the loop
- Per-subtask decomposition (which regulatory areas fail, and how)

Outputs land in `results/figures/` and feed the paper in `paper/`.
